Bataboom! turns your Apple Watch into a bat. Swing your arms like you're at the plate and the watch answers the instant you connect: the crack, the crowd, the call.

Thirty seconds of Bataboom!

  • Free Swing. Every swing is a hit, with exit velocity and distance. Back-to-back homers make the crowd louder.
  • HR Challenge. The pitch gets louder and peaks at the perfect moment. Early pulls it, late goes the other way.
  • Pass & Play. Two to four friends, one watch, five chances each.
  • iPhone companion. A chart of every round, where each hit landed, and your gear.
