How to play

  • Open Bataboom! on your Apple Watch and follow the short setup.
  • Hold your hands together like a bat, fingers interlaced, band snug.
  • Swing level and fast. The crack plays the moment you swing.
  • In HR Challenge, swing when the pitch sound is loudest.

Common questions

The screen goes dark when I swing.
That is normal when Always On is off. The game and the sound keep going; raise your wrist to see the result.

A swing did not make a sound.
Swing with both arms and turn around your body. Chopping down or pulling your arm back is ignored on purpose. You can also set Swing detection to High in Settings.

I can't hit home runs.
Distance comes from swing speed, not force: stay loose and swing all the way through. Start on Easy, where home runs need a slower swing. In Free Swing, the gold line on the bar is home-run speed.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
No. Your scores and unlocked items are backed up in your iCloud and on your paired iPhone, and come back when you reinstall.

Leaderboards don't show my score.
Open the Bataboom! app on your iPhone once while signed in to Game Center; it sends your best scores.

Safety

Clear the space around you, keep people and breakables out of reach, and tighten your watch band before you swing.
