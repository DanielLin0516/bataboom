Bataboom! guide

Pass & Play: a home run party on one watch

Pass & Play turns one Apple Watch into a party game. Two to four players take turns, five chances each, then the final standings and a victory cheer for the winner.

How to start

  1. Open Bataboom! on the watch and choose Pass & Play.
  2. Pick the number of Players (2 to 4), their Names and the Difficulty, then tap Start.
  3. When it says Pass the watch to a name, hand it over. That player tightens the band and taps Ready.
  4. After everyone has batted, tap See results for the Final standings.

Good to know

  • It plays like HR Challenge: listen for the pitch and swing when it is loudest.
  • Scores stay off your stats, leaderboards and unlocks, because several people share one watch.
  • Everyone uses the gear the watch has on.
  • Before each turn, clear the space and make sure the band is snug on the new wrist.
