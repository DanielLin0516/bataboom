Bataboom! guide

Locker Room: all 12 unlocks

The Locker Room has 12 items to unlock: 11 come from your HR Challenge results, and the Night Game from playing on three different days. Nothing is for sale. Gear changes the sound and the look, never the distance.

Bats

  • Wood BatYours from the start
  • Aluminum BatHit your first home run
  • Maple Pro25 home runs in total
  • Barrel Buster3 home runs in a row
  • Neon Bat100 home runs in total
  • Golden Bat10 home runs in one Pro round

Balls

  • Classic BallYours from the start
  • Glow BallFinish 5 rounds
  • FireballHit a home run of 480 ft
  • MoonshotHit a home run of 500 ft

Stadiums

  • SandlotYours from the start
  • Big League Park50 home runs in total
  • Night GamePlay on 3 different days

Laces

  • PlainYours from the start
  • Backwards Cap5 home runs in one round
  • Sweatband10 home runs in total

How unlocking works

  • Unlocks count HR Challenge results, except the Night Game, which counts days played in any mode.
  • Free Swing and Pass & Play home runs do not count.
  • New items appear after the round, one at a time. Tap Equip now or Later.
  • Every unlock is also a Game Center achievement.

Change your gear

Open Locker Room on the watch, or tap an item in the iPhone app to equip it on your watch; it applies when the watch app opens. Each bat has its own sound, and the Night Game brings the organ and fireworks.
